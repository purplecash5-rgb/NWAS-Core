# Corpus Policy

NWAS-Core is a framework repository. Bring your own lawful corpus.

This repository includes no third-party corpus and no private NWAS knowledge
package. It does not include a scraper, crawler, mass downloader, or corpus
extraction workflow.

The following are not published here:

- Private NWAS databases.
- Real profile layers.
- Real vector database files.
- Real embeddings.
- SQLite knowledge packages.
- Real evidence packets.
- Raw crawled content.
- NWT text.
- jw.org article text.
- EPUB, PDF, HTML, audio, or video source materials.

Allowed public content is limited to architecture, schemas, validators, policy
language, interfaces, toy synthetic examples, and short framework progress notes.
Downstream users are responsible for lawful corpus acquisition, licensing,
storage, and review.
